Chord Dock Editions

Chord Dock v2.0 comes in four editions: Lite (free) / Extended / Performance / Full Bundle. Extended goes deeper into harmony, Performance turns progressions into performance — neither one is “above” the other; they unlock different directions.

For every feature difference, see the full comparison table.

Edition Overview

Lite

The free edition for learning the Chord Dock workflow in your own DAW.

Free
  • Build chord progressions on the timeline
  • Organize arrangement ideas with Sections and Focus Edit
  • Export progression MIDI with drag and drop
  • Try the Arranger Mode demo (90 s of playback, Pads 1–3 per part)
  • Unlock any edition later with the same installer and a license key
Start Free with Lite

Extended

The harmony direction: deeper chords and transformation tools, same Lite workflow.

Paid
  • Compare and apply Reharmo ideas
  • Generate Arpeggio / Bassline from selected sections
  • Unlock tensions, slash chords, and Circle of Fifths tools
  • Gain finer edit controls and theme-side extras
See Extended Details

Performance

The performing direction: play your progression. New in v2.0.

NEW in 2.0
  • Arranger Mode (4 parts × 8 pads × 2 pattern sets)
  • Section Launcher with reservation-based switching
  • Performance / Song pages
  • Per-part MIDI output via ChordPart
See Performance Details

Full Bundle

Everything from Extended and Performance with a single license key.

Best Value
  • Harmony expansion and reharmonization (all of Extended)
  • Arranger Mode and ChordPart (all of Performance)
  • Free to move in either direction later
See Pricing & Buy
