What these guides focus on

How MIDI moves

The guides focus on MIDI handoff: when to drag out a clip, when to keep Chord Dock live in the session, and how to think about the routing without overcomplicating it.

What to test first

Lite is enough to verify the core loop. Once the host fit is clear, Extended becomes a speed upgrade for reharmonization, generated note material, and deeper harmonic editing.

Need to evaluate Chord Dock quickly?

Start with Lite, build a short progression, drag the MIDI into your session, and decide from that first loop whether the timeline-based harmony workflow is useful in your DAW.
